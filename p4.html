The Challenge

A hidden plant sits inside a closed loop with a PID controller. You cannot see its parameters. Tune Kp, Ki and Kd until the response meets every part of this spec, then submit your design.

Spec to meet:

      Controller Gains

      Open-Loop Step Test

      A noisy unit-step record of the plant on its own, with no controller. Read its shape (first or second order, final value, speed) the way you did in Module 4.

      Predict, then Check

      Predict how a gain change will affect the response. Then adjust a gain and compare your prediction with the simulation.

      Output vs Setpoint

      Green band: ±2% settling band around the setpoint. Orange dotted line: overshoot limit. Purple dotted line: settling-time limit. The output must be inside the green band before the purple line, and stay there.
